## Inference and limits

The source branch and runtime events independently support that this parameterized invocation bypasses result-index reuse and launches a rule JVM for each serial call, even while compilation results are cache hits. Iteration 2 stopped after 31 of the 32 process starts and had 30 completion-effect markers, so it shows progress ending partway through the loop. Iteration 4 had all 32 starts and 32 markers before its failed result; therefore “partway through 32” describes iteration 2, not both failures.

The lock intervals do not show a lock wait near the roughly 30-second body interval. These records establish repeated process launches and the point where observed progress ended; they do not identify why each launch path consumed that elapsed time, and they do not establish a host or buffer cause. The event offsets have millisecond resolution, and the review covers only these two failed iterations.
